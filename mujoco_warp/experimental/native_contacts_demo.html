A shared arena has a lifetime rule

Each group completes collision detection, constraint solving, integration, and its contact consumer before the next group reuses the arena. The after_group callback is the time to consume or record contacts. Earlier groups’ contact views become stale after reuse.

All users run in order on one device stream. Do not interleave step1/step2 across groups, reset data against stale contacts, or retain contact views as persistent input. This cannot keep every group’s contact set available simultaneously.

Direct native storage, fixed startup budget

The browser plays recorded real MuJoCo Warp positions and contact points with projected normals. Worlds keep 1, 4, or 12 spheres and reset drops every 128 steps. Objects stay fixed; contact counts change.

NativeContactTable specializes Warp’s VirtualMemory storage primitive. It uses no generic DynamicTable entity handles, deletion, compaction scratch, or contact snapshot copy. Native solver indices remain intact. Capacity is committed at startup; no allocation, growth, or implicit shrink occurs inside captured replay.
